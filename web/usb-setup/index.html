Wireless setup with Device Owner protection

Pair the child phone with your Windows computer over Wi-Fi. The setup downloads the latest official Companion, verifies its SHA-256, checks Android eligibility, and attempts Device Owner enrollment.

1 · Prepare

Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi. Install Android SDK Platform-Tools. On the phone, enable Developer options → Wireless debugging.

2 · Pair and enroll

Download and extract the setup kit. In PowerShell, open its folder and run the command below. Enter the connection IP:port, pairing IP:port and hidden six-digit pairing code when asked. The two ports can be different.

3 · Confirm protection

Wait for Verified: Companion is the Android Device Owner. Then open Companion, connect to your family and complete parent approval and permission checks.

Download wireless setup kit · Windows 
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\setup-managed-wireless.ps1" -AdbPath "C:\platform-tools\adb.exe" -Enroll

Replace the ADB path with your actual adb.exe location. Omit -Enroll for inspection without installing or assigning Device Owner. This uses a local ADB tool; browsers cannot directly perform this Wi-Fi pairing. Initial Device Owner enrollment needs an eligible phone: no existing accounts, extra users, managed profiles or another device owner. Android may still refuse enrollment. Nothing is reset and no accounts are removed. After setup, turn Wireless debugging off and forget the computer; managed protection may disable debugging automatically.
